Privacy Policy – History Shorts Upload

Last updated: 8 November 2026

“History Shorts Upload” is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the Echoes of Earth channel. It is not offered to the public and is used only with the owner's own accounts.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos (scope youtube.upload) to the owner's own channel. No other channel, user or viewer data is read.
  • TikTok and Instagram: permission to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or other users.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted (GitHub Actions secrets) and are used only to upload the owner's videos.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in the TikTok and Instagram app settings.
